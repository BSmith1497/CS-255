# CS-255
this is the final project I worked on while in CS 255 at SNHU
This project was to design a system for a company that was looking to help students improve their ability to pass their driving test. The client was the company DriverPass, more specifically, its owner, Liam. I feel I was able to determine the requirements for the system very well. One revision I would like to make would be to make the relationships clearer on the class diagram. I would move the items around to have less overlap with the lines that show relationships. To interpret the user’s needs, I revisited the interview that was completed with DriverPass whenever needed. Considering the user's needs is important because without understanding what the client wants, there is no way to build a system they will use.  
When designing software, I like to ensure that I fully understand the problem I am looking to solve and educate myself in whatever I may need. If I can put myself in the user's shoes I will be better able to develop the system if I know what it can be a user. In the future, the biggest strategy I will use is very frequent communication with the client. There is very little that is more valuable than direct feedback from the person I am analyzing or designing a system for.
